Key Features

  • URL-Addressed Rooms: Every path under /chat/{room} is an independent room you can bookmark or share with other active members.
  • WebRTC Voice Calls: Low-latency browser Opus voice chat backed by a Rust & GStreamer media pipeline.
  • Binary Protobuf & File Sharing: Stream messages and chunked file attachments with real-time upload and download progress over Protobuf WebSockets.
  • Persistent Wallet Nicknames: Reserve a unique lowercase nickname tied to your verified Ethereum wallet across all rooms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I invite people to a room?

Share any room URL such as /chat/my-team. Members who already have active paid access open the room directly; visitors without an active paid session are redirected to /chat?room=my-team to sign in with their Ethereum wallet and unlock access before entering the room.

Are messages and voice calls end-to-end encrypted?

No. Web and WebSocket traffic is protected in transit over HTTPS/WSS and WebRTC voice streams use DTLS-SRTP transport encryption in transit, but room messages, file chunks, and audio routing are processed by the server and are not end-to-end encrypted.

Are chat rooms indexed by search engines or made private by noindex?

Only the /chat overview page is publicly crawlable and listed in the sitemap. All /chat/{room} routes require an active paid session, redirect unauthenticated requests to onboarding, and emit a noindex robots directive. The noindex directive only tells compliant search crawlers not to list a URL in search results; it is not a privacy or room-access guarantee. Room access is enforced by server-side session and payment verification.
